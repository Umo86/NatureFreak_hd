About NATUREFreak

NATUREFreak is a nature-stories journal and a free directory of nature photographers, with paid featured posts on the Instagram and Facebook account @naturefreak_hd. The Instagram account has 146k followers. Every photo is credited to its photographer, and all paid features are labelled as paid.

We are happy to answer questions about how the directory works, how paid features are labelled, and how we credit photographers.

Get in touch

For press enquiries, use the form on the contact page and tell us which publication you are writing for and what you need. We will reply by email.

Please tell us your deadline if you have one, so that we can reply in time.
