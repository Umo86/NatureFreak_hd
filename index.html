Questions, answered.

Is the directory really free?

Yes. Being listed is free and it stays free. You never have to buy a feature.

Who can join?

Anyone who photographs nature, wildlife or landscapes. There is no follower minimum.

How soon is my listing live?

We review every listing before it goes live, then email you when it is up.

How do I pay for a feature?

You send the request and we confirm the slot by email with a secure payment link. Nothing is charged before that.

Do I keep the rights to my work?

Yes. Your photographs stay yours. Being listed or featured does not transfer anything to us.
