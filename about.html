About NATUREFreak

NATUREFreak is a home for nature stories and the people who photograph them.

What we do

NATUREFreak is three things in one place. First, a journal of nature stories about places, wildlife, oceans and photography. Second, a free directory of nature photographers, where anyone who photographs nature can be listed. Third, paid features on our Instagram and Facebook account, @naturefreak_hd, which has 146k followers on Instagram.

Credit where it is due

Every photo we show is credited to the person who took it. If you spot a photo that is missing a credit or has the wrong one, please tell us through the contact form and we will correct it.

Paid features

Photographers and brands can pay to have their work featured on @naturefreak_hd. Paid features are always labelled as paid, so you can tell them apart from the rest of what we post.

The directory and the journal are separate from this. Being listed in the directory is free and does not depend on buying anything.

Get in touch

If you have a question, a correction or an idea, use the form on the contact page. We read everything that comes in.
